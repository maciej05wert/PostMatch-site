Features

Everything you need to climb

PostMatch breaks down every ranked game into actionable coaching points so you know exactly what to work on.

🎯

Automated Coaching Flags

Every game is analysed for key mistakes — dying near objectives, late resets, unanswered objectives, poor vision control and more.

📊

Performance Scoring

Get a score and grade for every game based on role-specific metrics. Jungle, mid, top, ADC and support all scored differently.

📈

Champion & Matchup Stats

Track your win rate, KDA, CS/min and average score across all your champions and lane opponents over time.

📓

Game Journal

Write notes after every game — strong points, areas to improve, death reviews and goals for next game. Build real self-awareness.

🎬

VOD Review Sync

Link your recordings and jump directly to the timestamp of any flag. Review your mistakes in context without scrubbing through footage.

🔒

100% Local Storage

Your game data never leaves your machine. Reports are stored locally — no accounts, no servers, no data collection.

How it works

Simple. Fast. Actionable.

Enter Your Name

Type your Riot ID and select your region. PostMatch fetches your recent ranked games automatically.

2

Analyse

Click Analyze. PostMatch processes your last 40 games and caches them locally for instant access.

3

Review Flags

Open any game and see exactly what went wrong — timestamped flags with specific coaching advice.

4

Improve

Write your journal entry, set a goal for next game, and track your progress over time.
