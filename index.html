PostMatch

PostMatch is an all-in-one match analysis tool for League of Legends. Review your coaching flags, track long-term performance, and build better habits game by game.

The inspiration

Postmatch was born because I wanted to streamline the review process as much as possible. I wanted all the information I needed to learn and improve about the game all in one place, rather than having to have many sources of information open all at once. My hopes for PostMatch are to create a program where users from all levels of experience can open their match history and get a clearer idea of what they did well, what they could improve on and be able to do so over a long enough time to see reasonable progress.

Features

PostMatch covers your entire post-game process from raw stats to structured self-reflection.

Coaching Flags

Every game is automatically scanned for specific mistakes : dying near active objectives, missing resets, blind baron attempts, CS deficits, vision gaps and more. Each flag is timestamped so you know exactly when it happened.

Performance Scoring

Receive a score and letter grade for every game based on role-specific metrics. Jungle, top, mid, ADC and support are each evaluated against different standards because the game demands different things from each position.

Champion and Matchup Tracking

See your win rate, KDA, CS per minute and average score for every champion you play. Filter by time period and compare your performance against specific lane opponents over your last 40 ranked games.

Game Journal

Write structured notes after every game. Review your deaths one by one, record what you did well, identify what to improve and set a specific focus for next game. Self-review is one of the fastest ways to improve and PostMatch makes it a habit.

VOD Review Sync

Link your game recordings and jump directly to the timestamp of any coaching flag at the click of a button. No more scrubbing through 35 minutes of footage to find the moment that cost you the game.

LP History

PostMatch tracks your ranked LP over time and displays it as a graph so you can see your progression across both Solo Queue and Flex. Spot your slumps, your streaks and the patches that affected your climb.

How it works
1

Enter Your Riot ID

Type your Riot ID and select your region. PostMatch fetches your recent ranked games automatically using the Riot Games API.

2

Analyse Your Games

PostMatch processes up to 40 recent ranked games and builds a full coaching report for each one. Reports are cached locally so they load instantly on return visits.

3

Review Your Flags

Open any game and work through your coaching flags. Filter by category : deaths, vision, objectives, economy and jump to the moment in your VOD recording if you have one linked.

4

Build the Habit

Write your journal entry, set a goal for next game and track how your metrics trend over time. Improvement comes from consistency, and PostMatch is built to make that easy.

Role Support

Coaching flags and performance scoring are tailored to each role. What matters for a jungler is not what matters for a support.
