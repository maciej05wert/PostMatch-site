POSTMATCH

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Overview

PostMatch is built with privacy as a core principle. Your game data never leaves your machine. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why and how it is stored.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored exclusively on your local machine in your AppData folder and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Match history and game reports fetched from the Riot Games API
  • Performance scores and coaching flags generated by the Application
  • Journal entries and notes you write within the Application
  • Application settings including VOD folder path
  • LP history and rank snapshots

This data is stored at: %AppData%\PostMatch\ on your Windows machine. You can delete this data at any time by deleting that folder or using the built-in cache clear function within the Application.

3. Data We Collect Server-Side

If you create a PostMatch account, the following minimal data is stored on our secure servers:

  • Email address - used for account identification and login
  • Subscription status - free or premium tier
  • Linked Riot account identifiers (Riot ID and region) - used to verify account ownership
  • Monthly review usage count - used to enforce free tier limits

We do not store match data, reports, journal entries or any game-related content on our servers.

4. Riot Games API Data

PostMatch uses the Riot Games API to fetch match data. This data is retrieved on your behalf and stored locally on your device only. We do not log, store or analyse Riot API data on our servers. Data fetched includes:

  • Match history for your linked Riot accounts
  • Match details and timeline data for individual games
  • Ranked statistics and LP history

5. Data We Do Not Collect

PostMatch does not collect:

  • Your game data or match reports
  • Your journal entries or personal notes
  • Your IP address beyond what is required for API calls
  • Any behavioural or analytics data
  • Payment information - all payments are handled by Stripe

6. Third Party Services

PostMatch uses the following third party services:

  • Riot Games API - to fetch match and account data
  • Supabase - for account authentication and subscription management
  • Stripe - for payment processing. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data

7. Data Retention

Local data is retained until you delete it manually or use the cache clear function. Account data is retained until you delete your account. You can request account deletion at any time by contacting us through Discord or GitHub.

8. GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. Since we store minimal server-side data, a deletion request simply removes your account record from our database. Contact us through Discord to make a request.

9. Children's Privacy

PostMatch is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the Application after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us through our Discord server or GitHub repository.
